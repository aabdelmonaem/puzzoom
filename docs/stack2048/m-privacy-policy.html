Stack 2048 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Developer and scope

Ahmed Elbari, an individual developer based in Qatar, provides Stack 2048. In this policy, “I” and “the developer” mean Ahmed Elbari. For privacy questions or requests, email a.abdelmonaem@gmail.com and state the game name.

This policy covers the Stack 2048 mobile app for Android and iOS, including iPadOS, distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store. It does not cover browser or desktop versions of the game. App stores, system backups, and email services can involve other providers, as explained below.

2. Overview

Stack 2048 is a single-player sliding tile puzzle. No account, nickname, or real name is required. The current game has no advertising, in-app purchases, subscriptions, advertising trackers, developer-operated analytics, or integrated automatic crash-reporting service. It does not upload your board, scores, or preferences to a developer-operated game server. It has no online leaderboard, multiplayer feature, or developer-operated cloud-save service.

The game uses local data to resume play and remember preferences. Contact opens your email application; a message is sent only if you choose to send it. Normal gameplay and local saving work offline.

3. Local game data

The mobile app stores the following in application preferences on your device:

  • Current board: board size, tile values, tile identifiers and positions, score, game-started state, win status, and whether the 2048 milestone has been acknowledged.
  • Undo data: up to three prior board-and-score snapshots and the number of undo uses already spent.
  • Records: best score for each board size, games played, wins, lifetime move count, and highest tile. Win rate is calculated from those records.
  • Preferences: System default, Dark, or Light appearance; haptics; and animations.

These records support game functionality, continuity, and personal statistics. Tile identifiers identify pieces within a game, not you or your device. No advertising or persistent hardware identifier is created for gameplay. The Me screen is a menu, not an online profile. Earlier versions' unused preference fields can remain in an existing local save until it is rewritten; they are not uploaded.

4. Device controls

The game uses swipe and keyboard controls. It does not access motion sensors. Haptics use supported device vibration feedback, not microphone access or audio recording.

5. Offline gameplay and other providers

The installed game's normal puzzle logic and local saving do not require a developer-operated server. Fonts and artwork are bundled; runtime Google Fonts downloads are disabled. The game does not automatically send diagnostic logs to the developer.

App stores, operating-system vendors, and backup providers may independently handle downloads, system diagnostics, backups, or usage information according to their settings and policies. The developer may receive platform-provided reports if those platforms make them available under your sharing settings; that is distinct from an analytics or crash-upload feature built into the game.

6. Support communications

Choosing Contact opens a mailto link in an email application. The game does not read your inbox, access your contacts, attach your save data, or send an email automatically. If you send a message, the developer receives your email address, message, and any name, screenshots, logs, or other information you choose to include.

Support information is used to respond, investigate problems, handle privacy requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. Provide only what is needed to explain your issue. The support mailbox uses Gmail, and Google and your own email provider process messages under their respective policies. Their processing and storage can take place outside Qatar, including in other countries where they operate.

The developer does not sell personal information, use support messages for targeted advertising, or add you to a marketing list. Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to satisfy applicable law or a valid legal request, limited to what is required.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game records remain until overwritten or removed using your device's storage controls. Starting a new game replaces the active board and undo history but does not erase preferences, lifetime statistics, or best scores. Undo also does not delete lifetime records. There is no online account to delete and no developer-operated copy of your local save that the developer can remotely erase.

On Android, Clear storage or Clear app data removes local application records. On iOS, delete the app and its data; offloading an app can retain data.

Operating-system backups can retain or restore copies independently. Manage those backups through the relevant provider. Local records are not a guaranteed backup service, and storage can be lost through device failure, storage clearing, or app removal.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond to and document a request, resolve related issues or disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion by email. If information must be retained by law, the developer will explain the applicable restriction. Email-provider backup retention is governed by that provider.

8. Choices, privacy rights, and security

You can change preferences and remove local data as described above. For information held by the developer, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other rights available under applicable law. Where processing relies on consent, withdrawing consent does not undo earlier lawful processing. Only information reasonably needed to verify and handle a request will be requested.

You may raise concerns with the developer or the privacy authority available under the law that applies to you. Records stored only on your device normally have to be managed there. No extra identity document is needed simply to play the game.

The mobile app uses local application storage, not a secure vault. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed secure. Protect your device and backups.

9. General audience and children

Stack 2048 is a general-audience puzzle game, not a service specifically directed to children. It does not ask for a player's age, real name, or account. Parents and guardians should help younger players manage device permissions and support communications. If you believe a child sent personal information to the developer without any parental permission required by law, contact the support address so it can be investigated and deleted where required. Local processing and third-party platform practices described in this policy can still apply to younger players.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may change when the game's behavior, providers, or legal requirements change. A revised policy will show its updated date. Additional notice or consent will be obtained before materially different processing where required by law. Adding advertising, analytics, online accounts, cloud saves, or new data uses would require updated disclosures before introduction.
