The app

Mandala Studio is a creative drawing tool with Mandala Creator, Spirograph Creator, and Challenge modes. Mandala Creator uses rotating arms with adjustable lengths and speeds. Spirograph Creator traces patterns with two or three nested rings and wheels. You can adjust playback speed, guide visibility, line thickness, and pen color in Mandala Creator and Spirograph Creator, pause and resume playback, and reset the drawing. Challenge Mode offers pattern-matching puzzles for both Mandala Creator and Spirograph Creator. Choose a track on the challenge selection screen to open its level screen. Each track has 100 fixed numbered levels in 10 chapters, with editable geometry controls in every level, increasingly complex patterns, and higher match goals. Drawing guides remain visible through completion. Matching the target at the required score unlocks the next level. Best scores and unlocks are saved on your device, and unlocked levels can be replayed. Clearing app data or browser storage removes progress. Platform backups may restore it according to your device settings. There is no account-based synchronization or publisher-operated progress recovery service. The app is intended for creative and recreational use; it does not provide medical advice or treatment.

Sharing drawings

After a drawing is complete, you can preview a PNG image of the canvas and choose Share PNG. The exported image excludes drawing arms, rings, wheels, navigation controls, and toolbars. The screenshot feature renders the app's own canvas; it does not use your device camera. Export creates a static image, not an editable project or saved configuration.

On supported platforms, share_plus opens a share interface so you can choose an app, recipient, or destination. On the web, the browser may download the image if file sharing is unavailable. Image sharing is not supported on Linux. Sharing can be canceled, blocked, or fail because of platform restrictions or receiving-app compatibility. Opening or dismissing a share sheet does not guarantee delivery or saving; check the receiving app or destination yourself.
