Mandala Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Overview

This policy explains how Ahmed Elbari handles information in the Mandala mobile app. It covers Mandala Studio, Spirograph (including its three-ring option), Challenge Mode, and image sharing in the current app. Separately hosted websites, browser or hosting-provider request logs, and the app stores that distribute the app are outside this policy; their operators are responsible for their own disclosures.

Information handled by the app

Mandala does not require an account. The current app does not collect or transmit personal information to the publisher and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party crash-reporting services.

The app calculates geometric patterns on your device. Drawing points, axis lengths and rotation speeds, Spirograph ring and wheel settings, pen colors and thickness, guide visibility, playback state, challenge targets, scores, and unlocked levels are held temporarily in memory to provide the drawing experience. The app does not automatically save drawings or settings or upload them to a server.

Sharing drawings

The Share button becomes available after a drawing finishes. It creates a PNG image from the app's drawing canvas in memory; it does not use your device camera or capture other apps, navigation controls, or toolbars. Exported images exclude drawing arms, rings, and wheels.

You can preview the image before choosing Share PNG. The app uses share_plus to pass the PNG to your platform's share interface. You choose the receiving app, person, or service. On the web, supported browsers use the Web Share API; otherwise the image may be downloaded. Image sharing is not supported on Linux.

Sharing may create temporary image files in the app's cache. These files can remain after the share sheet is dismissed and may be removed by the operating system as part of cache management. The app does not upload images to a publisher-operated server. Dismissing the share sheet does not guarantee deletion of temporary files.

A receiving app or service may transmit, store, or further share an image according to its own terms, privacy practices, and your selected recipients or destination. The publisher does not control those services or copies once shared.

Device permissions

The current app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, or location. Sharing a PNG uses the platform share interface or browser download mechanism. The app does not require broad access to your files or photo library for this feature. Any receiving app may request permissions under its own practices.

Retention and deletion

Temporary drawing and challenge data is discarded when the app process ends. Reset clears the current drawing while keeping the current board's settings. Drawing settings may also be discarded when you leave a board; challenge scores and unlocked levels last only for the current home-screen session. Closing a screen or placing the app in the background does not necessarily end the app process. There is no account or publisher-operated database of drawings to delete. Shared or downloaded PNG files are separate from the temporary drawing session. Resetting a drawing, leaving a board, or uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete those files. Delete downloaded images using your file manager or browser downloads, and manage shared copies with the receiving app, recipient, or service, including any provider-managed backups or deleted-items folders where applicable. The publisher cannot retrieve or delete copies held by others.

Sharing and platform services

The app does not sell drawing or usage data or send it to advertising or analytics services. Apart from an export you choose as described above, it does not send your drawings to third parties. Your operating system and app store may independently process download, purchase, diagnostic, or device information under their own settings and privacy policies. This policy does not describe their separate practices.

Contacting us

If you choose to contact us by email, we receive the information you provide, including your email address and message. We use it to respond to your request and address support or privacy issues. Email providers process messages to deliver and store that correspondence. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact a.abdelmonaem@gmail.com to ask about access, correction, or deletion of correspondence. We will handle requests as required by applicable law.

Children

The current drawing experience does not ask users, including children, to provide personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact a.abdelmonaem@gmail.com so we can review and address it.

Security

Drawing calculations take place on your device. Use your device's security features to protect access to it. No device, communication method, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. The revised policy will show a new effective date. If we introduce data collection or permissions in a future version, we will update the disclosures and obtain consent where required before that processing begins.
